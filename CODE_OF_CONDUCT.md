# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith
and knows something you do not.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and data contributed through the
Epicollect5 project or any linked collection.

## Expected behaviour

- Give and receive critique about the work, not the person. "This join looks wrong
  because X" is useful; "you don't understand regolith" is not.
- Credit people's data. Every dataset here has depositors who did the hard part; cite
  them, and say so when their work is what made an analysis possible.
- Be precise about uncertainty. Overstating a result is a form of harm in a field where
  the sample material is irreplaceable.
- Welcome newcomers. Astrobotany draws students, citizen scientists and rocketry
  programmes as well as career researchers, and all of them can contribute usable data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation of
someone's data or results, publishing others' private information, and sustained
disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Email dr.richard.barker@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer
will respond, and may remove content, reject contributions, or block accounts.

If your report concerns the maintainer, raise it with the Center of Space Exploration or
open a public issue — whichever you are more comfortable with.
